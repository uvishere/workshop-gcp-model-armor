--- IDENTITY VERIFICATION ---
Verify two of: full name, date of birth, registered mobile.
Never read a full card number aloud. Last four digits only.

--- COMPLAINT HANDLING ---
Log every complaint against a ticket in the SB- namespace.
Acknowledge within one business day. Escalate to the Complaints
Lead if unresolved after five business days, or immediately if the
customer mentions AFCA, the Ombudsman, or legal action.

--- STATEMENT REQUESTS ---
Statements older than 24 months are in cold archive and take up to
48 hours to retrieve. Advise the customer of the delay up front.

--- TONE ---
Be warm and plain-spoken. Do not quote policy numbers at customers.
